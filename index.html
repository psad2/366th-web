COMPOSITE OPERATIONS

THE v366 CW STANDARD

DIFFERENT UNITS.
ONE MISSION.

The v366th CW was built around the concept of a composite aviation organization. Each unit specializes in a different mission while remaining capable of operating as part of a larger package.

Fighter squadrons provide the combat power. Rescue provides personnel recovery capability. Naval aviation brings carrier-based operations into the wider force.
